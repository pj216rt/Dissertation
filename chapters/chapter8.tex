The following procedure generates smooth realization of regression coefficient functions 
(typically referred to as \(\beta\)) for three dimensions.  A B-spline basis provides a flexible yet 
smooth representation of a function, while assigning random normal coefficients allows for subject specific variability 
around the conditional mean function\footnote{A B spline basis allows for a smooth functional form.  
A penalty matrix controls how smooth this functional form is.}.  For each of the three response dimensions, 
we construct B-spline basis functions 
over an evenly spaced grid and multiply these by a matrix of randomly drawn 
coefficients.  The resulting \(p\) curves for each dimension are concatenated to form 
a final \(p \times 3D\) matrix.
