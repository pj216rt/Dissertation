While there is a plethora of literature available on \say{traditional} FDA, the emphasis of this work is on hierarchical FDA, 
or Functional Mixed Models (FMM) specifically.  
As stated before, non functional mixed models have existed for decades.  
However oftentimes these assume a parametric form.  
A good review of FMM comes from Fine et al., where she compares functional
 non parametric mixed models to parametric models\cite{fine_examination_2019}.
